## Version 2.0 (planned on 2026-10-29)

The owner's direction: a more professional version (reliable, a richer app, a polished presentation, open to contributors), with WASM. **The first 2.0 must get reliability right**: a standalone WASM module of the `msfs-ui` package keeps the mod's data in a file of its `\work` folder, written to the disk at once, on Steam and on the Microsoft Store edition (it would settle 1.5). Then the app connected to the sim (SimConnect and the CommBus: the user's data shown, edited, exported and imported). Must not happen: the sim slower, even a little. The 1.x data may be lost at the move to 2.0 (the owner's decision). Developed on a branch, published in place of 1.x.

A standalone module of a Community package starts (`Ready` in `AsoboReport-RunningSession.txt`) only when it exports the memory functions of the SDK's `libc.a` (`malloc` … `mark_decommit_pages`): without them, `Failed`, even empty; the version functions of `MSFS_WasmVersions.a` are not needed. The command-line build that works: `clang-cl --driver-mode=g++` (its MSVC flags are ignored for wasm) and `wasm-ld` with those exports and the function table. A package with an MSFS 2024 manifest (`builder`) gets its folder in `%APPDATA%\Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024\WASM\MSFS2024\<package>\`, its `work` folder there; `"\\work\\…"` and `"/work/…"` reach it. Files written in a session were read back after a Quit and a restart. Reading needs the exact size (`fsIOOpenRead` with a larger count fails; the open-then-size way gave no callback). Menu ↔ module: 10 B to 1 MB, 12 to 22 ms, no change in the menu's frame cadence; there is no `Coherent` in the main menu (`engine.*`), and other add-ons' events reach the same listener. App ↔ module and app ↔ menu over SimConnect: both ways work (80 to 110 ms), through the native `SimConnect.dll` (the managed wrapper is .NET Framework and does not load in .NET 10); a message above 40 to 50 KB crashes `SimConnect.dll` in the app: chunks of 32 KB at most. **For step 1**: the package's manifest gains the MSFS 2024 fields (`SimPackageFiles`); the store's format tells the reader its size; the app's protocol is chunked | |

### 6a in detail: favourite and recent airports

**What.** On Free Flight, under the aircraft favourites of the left column, an *Airports* section:
- one row per favourite airport and per recent departure: ICAO code, name and city;
- a click on a row sets the airport as the departure; a small button sets it as the arrival;
- the sim's heart on the current departure's row adds it to the favourites or removes it, as for the aircraft.

**Exploration in the sim first** (sim running; every change put back afterwards):
1. `ON_DEPARTURE_AIRPORT_SELECTED(index, database)` really sets the departure (read in the sim's code, never triggered yet);
2. a search for an exact ICAO (`LFBZ`) returns that airport first, or needs `SetSearchParams`;
3. what happens when the sim's own drop-down list is open at that moment.

**Code.**
- `mod/src/sim/airport-search.ts`: search, wait for the exact ICAO, select, wait for the sim's confirmation (`departure.worldLocation.iCAO`), with a bounded wait;
- `mod/src/features/favorite-airports/`, made of the sim's components (`Marketplace_Tile_Horizontal` rows, `IconButton_Light_Wishlist` heart, `real-scroll`), with its pure parts tested;
- `features.json` (feature `favoriteAirports` and its options) and `compat.json` (listener, events, paths, components);
- data in the sim's storage: `MSFS_UI_FAVORITE_AIRPORTS`, `MSFS_UI_RECENT_DEPARTURES`;
- the app shows the new feature on its Features page by itself; only the shared `config.js` fixtures are generated again.

**Open questions for the user.**
- Placement: a second section under the aircraft favourites, or *Aircraft* / *Airports* tabs in the same card?
- Options: show the recent departures or not, and how many (0 to 10, 5 by default)?
- The *arrival* button on each row: now, or the departure only at first?
